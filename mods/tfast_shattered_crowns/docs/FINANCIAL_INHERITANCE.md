# Financial inheritance

Status: POSTPONED. No financial-succession script is in the mod.

County gold workings follow the county. Vanilla title succession already moves them. That is the only inheritance behavior Phase 1 has, and it is the ordinary game, not a new system.

A separate banking heir, mining heir, share split, or charter would need the banking ledger from Phase 4 first. It is not implemented, and it must not be faked by duplicating gold on death.
